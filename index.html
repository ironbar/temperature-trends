How to read it

Maximum plot: at any temperature, the line height is the number of days that reached that temperature or higher.

Minimum plot: at any temperature, the line height is the number of days that fell to that temperature or lower.

Averaging: each line is centred on its labelled year. A 3-year line averages the previous, labelled, and following years.
